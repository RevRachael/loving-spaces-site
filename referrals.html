For Referral Partners

Thank you for thinking of Loving Spaces for the women you serve. Here is what to know before you refer.

Who we serve

Loving Spaces offers safe, supportive independent living for senior women aged 55 and over who are facing unfortunate circumstances. Many have nowhere else to go, whether a roommate or family situation is no longer working, they lost their home during a hospital stay, or the loss of a loved one has made their current home unaffordable.

What kind of housing this is

This is entirely independent living. Loving Spaces does not provide medical care and does not have 24/7 on site staff, so a resident must be able to manage her own medications and personal care. Residents are welcome to have home health or hospice services, which they arrange on their own.

How a referral works

Referrals come from senior marketers, social workers, and case managers. Each prospective resident has an in person interview, and we ask for proof of income to confirm she can comfortably afford the rent. Every applicant is held to the same written criteria.

What we look for

We look for a woman who takes ownership of her situation, is open to support, and is seeking solutions. She should be willing to live in a supportive, community oriented environment, and we take time to explain the mission of Loving Spaces so she knows exactly what she is joining.

How to refer someone

Send us a message with a few words about the woman you have in mind and the best way to reach you, and we will be in touch. You are also welcome to visit. We love for referral partners to see the housing in person.
