Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question to see the answer.

Who is Loving Spaces for?

Loving Spaces offers safe, supportive living for senior women aged 55 and over who are facing unfortunate circumstances. Many of the women we serve have nowhere else to go, whether a roommate or family situation is no longer working, they lost their home during a hospital stay, or the loss of a loved one has made their current home unaffordable. A few come to us from elder abuse situations.

What are you looking for in a resident?

We look for a woman who takes ownership of her situation, is open to support, and is seeking solutions. We also ask whether she is willing to live in a supportive, community oriented environment, and we take time to explain the mission of Loving Spaces so she knows exactly what she is joining.

What kind of housing is Loving Spaces?

We are independent living housing for senior women who want to maintain their independence while enjoying the warmth of community. We are not an assisted living, group home, or medical facility.

Who is this housing best suited for?

Our housing is ideal for senior women who are mostly independent, but appreciate companionship and a supportive, peaceful living environment.

Is medical care provided?

This is entirely independent living, and Loving Spaces does not provide medical care. Residents who receive home health or hospice services are welcome, and they arrange and coordinate that care on their own. It does not interfere with their agreement with Loving Spaces, and we work respectfully alongside outside providers to keep the environment peaceful and comfortable.

What services are included?

We offer fully furnished private or shared rooms. Residents also enjoy shared living areas and a calm, home-like setting.

What does the rent include?

Monthly rent at Loving Spaces includes all utilities (electricity, water, gas, and trash), a private or shared bedroom, full access to all shared living spaces, a peaceful, well-maintained housing environment, and a kind and supportive community of women. There are no hidden fees, just one monthly rate for a simple, supportive lifestyle.

Can residents bring their own furniture or personal items?

Yes! While rooms are fully furnished, residents are welcome to personalize their space with decorations, photos, or small furniture pieces. If preferred, we’re happy to remove our furnishings to make room for yours.

Are pets allowed?

We do not have an open door pet policy, but small pets may be allowed on a case by case basis, depending on their temperament and compatibility with the housing. If you have a beloved companion animal, we are happy to talk and see if it is a good fit.

Are visitors allowed?

Yes, friends and family are always welcome to visit. We simply ask that all guests are respectful of the quiet, communal nature of our housing and the comfort of fellow residents.

Is staff on site 24/7?

No, we do not have 24/7 on site staff. This is independent living housing, and residents must be capable of managing their own medications and personal care.

Are there organized activities?

We don’t have a formal activity schedule. Residents are encouraged to engage with one another naturally, chatting in the living room, enjoying hobbies, or taking quiet time in their own rooms.

How do I know if this is the right fit?

If you are a senior woman who values independence, but would enjoy a peaceful, supportive community, this could be the perfect place for you. We are happy to talk with you and help determine if it is a good fit.

How do I schedule a visit or learn more?

Call us at (725) 277-2486 or send an email to LVlovingspaces@gmail.com. We’d love to welcome you for a tour, answer your questions, and help you explore what life at Loving Spaces could look like.

How much does it cost to live at Loving Spaces?

Our pricing varies depending on the type of room you’re interested in. We offer both private and shared rooms. Since everyone’s needs and preferences are a little different, we’d love to talk with you directly to go over the options and find the best fit. Please call us at (725) 277-2486 or email us at LVlovingspaces@gmail.com.
