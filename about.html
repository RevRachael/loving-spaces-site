About Us

Mission Statement

Loving Spaces is dedicated to offering safe, supportive living environments for senior women aged 55 and over who are experiencing challenging circumstances. Our mission is to provide a compassionate space where women can regain stability, heal emotionally and physically, and feel protected in an environment that respects their dignity.

We focus on assisting women transitioning from difficult situations, such as elder abuse shelters or hospice care, by creating a nurturing community that supports their mental and physical well-being.

Our Las Vegas Independent Housing Includes:

Private or Shared Rooms: Fully furnished rooms for comfortable everyday living.

Companionship and Community: A warm, welcoming atmosphere where friendships thrive.

Peaceful Location: A quiet, calm place to call home.

Our housing is ideal for senior women who value their independence, but appreciate the comfort of community and support.
